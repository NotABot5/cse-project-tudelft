# Meeting 2

#### Agenda

Quick Summary: Good

Feedback:

* The agenda was uploaded on time and it followed the template, but it is useful if you sectionize the talking points. If there are talking points focusing on a similar theme, there can be some nested bullet points. Extra explanation on very important points can also be helpful.
* The time estimation is there to give a rough idea of where you should be in your meeting flow, but it is completely natural for estimations to be off. The important thing is to try to learn from previous meetings about which kinds of issues take more time, and improve your estimation skills.

#### Chair performance

Quick Summary: Good

Feedback:

* The chair followed the agenda closely and led the meeting. The chair made sure everybody spoke their mind, but if some members are too quiet, the chair can address them and ask for their input (if necessary and beneficial).

#### Attitude & Relation

Quick Summary: Good

Feedback:

* The team took full ownership of the meeting; everybody tried to be active. If anyone has a constructive opinion, they should state it, and the team must foster an environment in which everybody is encouraged to contribute.
* Make sure everybody is on the same page with the points made and the matters discussed. If you state something that might not be trivial for the rest of the team, make sure it has been understood and agreed upon or discussed. (The chair is also partly responsible for this as well.)

#### Work Contribution/Distribution in the Team

Quick Summary: Good

Feedback:

* The division of tasks was discussed with everybody, and preferences were asked. Make sure you also take into account teammates’ schedules during task division (whose work relies on whom, how vital each part is, how heavy the task is, and whether everybody meets their quota, a personal matter that you should mostly keep track of individually).
* If you feel a task is heavy, feel free to ask for assistance from teammates.
